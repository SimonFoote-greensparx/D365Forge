Profile builder

Describe your organisation, its goals, its current systems and the team, then download a profile for the project-tailoring department. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

After you download it: save the profile in your own OneDrive (for Copilot Cowork) or in the input/ folder of your copy of the repository. Then attach it and ask: "Validate this profile, recommend the edition, propose the role mapping for me to confirm, then build the project pack." The pack is built only after you have confirmed the edition and who holds each role, so that no document is built on guesses.
