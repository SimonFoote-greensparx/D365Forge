# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming project for CRM practitioners at every level of experience.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the content, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Do not share confidential client or employer information, in issues, discussions or pull requests.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation from people
who repeatedly ignore it. Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `AUTHORS.md`.
